Privacy Policy

Last updated: Feb 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: We collect your email address purely for the purpose of creating your account, tracking your Razorpay top-ups, and managing your consumed AI generation minutes.

Payment Information: All payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We do not store, process, or view your credit card details, UPI IDs, or banking credentials.

2. What We Do NOT Collect (System Diagnostics & Privacy)

LiveAI Stealth Copilot operates entirely via local Windows interception. We do not:

  • Record, save, or store your desktop screen or visual data on our servers.
  • Store transcripts of your live interviews permanently. Audio buffers are streamed directly to our speech-to-text API partners (Deepgram), converted to text, fed to Groq for generation, and immediately discarded from our active memory once the WebSocket session concludes.
  • Sell or distribute your email address to third-party marketers.

3. Third-Party Services

By using LiveAI Stealth Copilot, your data (audio streams, resume text, and job descriptions) is temporarily processed by our critical infrastructure partners:

  • Supabase: For secure database management and user credit ledgering.
  • Razorpay: For secure payment processing.
  • Deepgram: For real-time, low-latency audio transcription.
  • Groq: For rapid LLM inference.

These partners are bound by their own enterprise privacy agreements and do not use your real-time interview data to train public models.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
