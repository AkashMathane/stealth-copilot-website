Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: Feb 28, 2026

1. Cancellation Policy

LiveAI Stealth Copilot operates on a strict, pre-paid "Pay-As-You-Go" model. There are no recurring subscriptions or auto-renewals associated with your account. Because there is no subscription, there is no service to "cancel." You simply top up your account with computing minutes when needed, and stop when you do not need them anymore.

2. Refund Policy for Consumed Credits

All purchases of AI compute time (minutes) are final and non-refundable once purchased.

Because LiveAI Stealth Copilot immediately expends resources utilizing premium, third-party APIs (Groq and Deepgram) to provide real-time latency during your live interviews, we incur absolute and immediate costs for every second you stream on our platform. Therefore, we cannot issue refunds for minutes that have already been purchased and added to your wallet, regardless of whether you have fully depleted that wallet balance yet.

Please utilize the 10-minute Free Trial provided to every new account to thoroughly test the application's compatibility with your computer hardware and microphone before purchasing additional time.

3. Service Outages & Failed Top-Ups

If a Razorpay transaction is successful and money is debited from your bank account, but our backend fails to accurately credit the API minutes to your LiveAI account ledger within 24 hours due to a technical server failure, we will either manually inject the missing credits sequence or refund the exact transaction amount back to the original payment method.
