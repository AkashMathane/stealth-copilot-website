How HelpingHand Solves Your Problems

  1. Install & Launch
    Download the lightweight Windows application. It runs quietly and smoothly without interrupting your workflow.
  2. Provide Context
    Click the Settings icon ⚙️ to feed the AI your current project details, notes, or background information. HelpingHand instantly adapts to understand your specific situation.
  3. Unobtrusive Overlay
    Press Ctrl + \ to activate the transparent interface. HelpingHand sits seamlessly over your screen, allowing you to click through it and use your computer normally.
  4. Real-Time Guidance
    Hold Space while the conversation is happening. HelpingHand's advanced AI instantly processes the context and displays the perfect response or insight right before your eyes.
  5. Hide Instantly
    Need your screen completely clear? Tap Ctrl + \ to instantly hide the overlay. You are always in total control.
