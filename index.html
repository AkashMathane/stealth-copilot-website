How to Use LiveAI Stealth Copilot

  1. Install the Desktop App
    Download and run the Windows installer. The app will boot invisibly as 'System Diagnostics' to avoid suspicion in your Task Manager.
  2. Configure Your Persona (RAG)
    Click the Gear icon ⚙️ to paste your Resume and the Job Description you are interviewing for. The AI will perfectly adapt its tone to your background.
  3. Ghost Mode Activation
    Press Ctrl + \ to toggle the Copilot's visibility. The entire app window becomes "transparent" to your mouse—you can click straight through it to interact with Zoom or Teams natively!
  4. Live Interviewing
    Hold Space while the interviewer speaks to stream their voice to the cloud. The AI will instantly print the perfect localized answer to your overlay.
  5. Emergency Panic Button
    If asked to share your entire screen, do not panic! The app is hard-coded to ignore screen captures. However, if you need to hide it immediately, tap Ctrl + \.
